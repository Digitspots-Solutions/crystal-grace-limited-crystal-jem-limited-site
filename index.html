About Oil Technologies

Oil Technologies Refining Solutions Limited, also operating as Oil Technologies Process Engineering Co, is a premier engineering firm specializing in oil and gas refinery solutions. Based in Egbeda Local Government, Nigeria, we bring deep technical expertise and industry knowledge to every project.

Our team is led by experienced refinery process engineers who understand the complexities of hydrocarbon processing, distillation systems, catalytic operations, and process optimization. We are committed to delivering solutions that meet international standards while addressing the unique challenges of the Nigerian oil and gas sector.

With a foundation built on technical excellence and operational integrity, we partner with refineries, petrochemical plants, and energy companies to enhance their processing capabilities and achieve sustainable operational performance.

Process Engineering Solutions

Refinery Process Design

Comprehensive design and engineering services for crude oil distillation units, catalytic cracking systems, and hydroprocessing facilities. We develop efficient process configurations that maximize yield and product quality.

Process Optimization & Troubleshooting

Advanced analysis and optimization of existing refinery operations. Our engineers identify bottlenecks, improve efficiency, and resolve complex operational challenges to enhance overall plant performance.

Technical Consultancy

Expert advisory services covering process safety, equipment selection, regulatory compliance, and technology evaluation. We provide strategic guidance for refinery modernization and capacity expansion projects.

Operational Training & Support

Specialized training programs for operations personnel, process engineers, and technical staff. We transfer knowledge and build local capacity for sustainable refinery operations.

Project Management

End-to-end project delivery for refinery construction, modification, and turnaround projects. Our structured approach ensures on-time, on-budget completion while maintaining the highest safety standards.
